📖 How each tab works

1. Get a crop rotation recommendation (the core tool)

NASA RECOMMENDATIONS is the default page. Click the map (or use your location, or type coordinates) to choose your farm, pick a historical year, your current crop, and a priority, then GET REAL NASA RECOMMENDATIONS. This calls the project's real engine: live NASA POWER weather and ISRIC SoilGrids soil data, scored and ranked for that exact location. "Historical year" means a specific past calendar year of actual recorded weather (2001–2024), not a forecast — it's the representative year the engine scores against.

2. Double-check a rotation by crop family

ROTATION lets you check a current crop plus recent crops for same-family pest/disease risk, or send the same rotation to the real NASA engine for a historical ranking (requires a location, same as above).

3. Explore the crops and your impact

CROP CARDS shows the real crops the engine can recommend. SDG BADGES shows which UN Sustainable Development Goals your last recommendation supports.

4. Keep farming records

In TRACKER, create or select a farm before starting a crop record. You can edit the crop and field notes later and mark a crop as harvested. Growth estimates are rough. Check local soil tests and professional agricultural guidance before acting on recommendations. DASHBOARD gives an overview of your saved fields and plantings.

5. Play the optional minigame

GAME is a pixel-battle minigame: enter a farm's details, fight a climate "threat" chosen by your biome/season/soil/water inputs (not random), then winning takes you straight into a real NASA recommendation for that farm. JOURNAL tracks monsters you've defeated; SAVED keeps field plans and lets you reset progress. All of this is stored in this browser only, not on a server, so it won't follow you to another device.

6. Start again when you want

In SAVED, use RESET GAME PROGRESS to clear your XP, victories, battles, game journal, and unlocked plans while keeping your fields, plantings, and activity logs. Alternatively choose RESET EVERYTHING for this profile, including farming records. Both require confirmation and cannot be undone. These actions do not affect other profiles.
